## Tour Schedules — % Tourya en slots (solo backoffice)
## Request: slotPercentageTourya en PUNTOS (15 = 15%)
## GET: slotPorcentajeTourya en PUNTOS (solo con token backoffice)
## NO enviar % en POST/PUT /tour-schedules/config
## Eliminado: PUT /tour/admin/porcentajeTourya/{tourId}
## =========================

# % masivo por rango de fechas (actualiza todos los slots de schedules en el rango + recalcula price)
curl --location --request PUT "{{baseUrl}}/tour-schedules/tours/36/percentage" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer {{tokenBackoffice}}" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data-raw '{
    "slotPercentageTourya": 15,
    "startDate": "2026-05-30",
    "endDate": "2026-06-02"
  }'
